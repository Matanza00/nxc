      GaragePal

      GaragePal Auto-Service Marketplace, Mobile App and Garage Dashboard

      ProductAuto-service marketplace
      IndustryAutomotive
      Year2025
      PlatformMobile app and web
      Livegaragepal.ca

      Overview

      GaragePal connects drivers with vetted garages in Canada and Qatar. Drivers find a nearby garage, book a service and track their car in the app; garage owners run bookings, staff, customers and messages from a web dashboard.

      The challenge

      Booking car service still meant phone calls and guesswork about price and timing. The marketplace needed a driver app simple enough for a quick booking and a garage dashboard strong enough to run a workshop day.

      Delivered as: Lead full-stack developer · Built at Nexifies

      problem / solution

      What we set out to fix

      Problem

      • Drivers could not compare garages, prices or availability.
      • Once a car was dropped off, its status was a phone call away.
      • Garages juggled bookings, staff and customers across tools.
      2countries, Canada and Qatar
      2products: a driver app and a garage dashboard
      15core screens across both

      Solution

      • Search nearby vetted garages on a map, with reviews.
      • Book a service and follow the job live in the app.
      • A garage dashboard for bookings, appointments, staff and customers.
      • In-app messaging between drivers and garages.

      process

      How we built it

      01

      Research

      Mapped the booking journey from both sides of the counter.

      02

      UX design

      A three-step booking for drivers, a day view for garages.

      features

      What it does

      feature 01

      Find a garage you can trust

      • Search vetted garages nearby on a live map.
      • Compare services, reviews and availability before you book.

      feature 02

      Book it, track it, pay for it

      • Book a service in a few taps and follow the job live.
      • Pay and chat with the garage without leaving the app.

      feature 03

      A workshop day, organised

      • Bookings, appointments, staff and customers in one garage dashboard.
      • Messages with drivers kept next to the job they belong to.

      stack.md

      The tech stack, and why we used it

      Next.jsOne framework for the garage dashboard and the marketing pages.
      Node.js, MySQL and PrismaBookings, staff and invoices are relational, and one backend serves the app and the dashboard.
      Google MapsDrivers already understand it, so finding a nearby garage needs no explaining.
      OAuth and 2FAQuick sign-in for drivers and stronger protection for garage accounts.
      Tailwind CSSKeeps 15 core screens consistent across both products.

      users.md

      Who uses it

      Drivers in Canada and Qatar

      Find a vetted garage, book it and follow the job live.

      Garage owners and staff

      Run bookings, appointments, staff and customers from one dashboard.

      benefits.md

      Benefits

      • Drivers compare services, reviews and availability before booking.
      • Job status replaces a phone call to the garage.
      • Messages stay next to the job they belong to.

      cost.md

      Cost benefits

      • One backend behind both products means one system to build and maintain.
      • Fewer phone calls for garage staff, and bookings kept in one tool.
