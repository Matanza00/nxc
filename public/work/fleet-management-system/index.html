      Fleet Management

      National Fleet Management System, Dashboard UX and Front-End

      ProductNational fleet platform
      IndustryLogistics
      Year2024
      PlatformWeb
      Delivered asLead front-end developer, SOS Globals

      Overview

      A national fleet-management system for 580+ vehicles across 12+ cities in Pakistan. Live tracking, drivers, fuel, maintenance, insurance and emergency jobs live in one platform, with role-based access from regional heads down to drivers.

      The challenge

      Fleet data was split across spreadsheets, phone calls and paper logs. Head office could not see where vehicles were, fuel losses went unnoticed, and every role needed a different view of the same operation.

      problem / solution

      What we set out to fix

      Problem

      • No live view of where 580+ vehicles were, or why they had stopped.
      • Fuel anomalies and maintenance were caught late, on paper.
      • Regional heads, supervisors, technicians and drivers shared one blunt view.
      35%higher employee efficiency
      38%lower fuel expenses

      Solution

      • Live tracking, filterable by moving, idle, workshop or parked.
      • Fuel logs with anomaly flags and cost per litre.
      • Scheduled maintenance, emergency jobs and insurance to closure.
      • Role-based access for four regional heads and every team.

      process

      How we built it

      01

      Research

      Mapped how the fleet runs day to day: dispatch, fuel, workshops and incidents.

      02

      UX design

      Designed one dashboard per role over a shared data model.

      03

      UI design

      A dense, calm interface: condensed numbers, clear status colours.

      04

      Build

      React and Next.js on Node.js, MySQL and Prisma, with real-time APIs.

      stack.md

      The tech stack, and why we used it

      React and Next.jsOne dashboard for each role, built from shared components over one data model.
      Node.js with real-time APIsPushes vehicle status to the map as it changes, instead of a daily report.
      MySQL and PrismaFuel, maintenance and job records are relational, so a strict database and typed queries fit them.
      Tailwind CSSKeeps a dense, data-heavy interface consistent across many screens.

      users.md

      Who uses it

      Head office

      See 580+ vehicles live across 12+ cities and catch problems as they happen.

      Four regional heads and supervisors

      Run their region from a view built for their role.

      Technicians and drivers

      See their own jobs, schedules and assignments.

      benefits.md

      Benefits

      • 35% higher employee efficiency.
      • Fuel anomalies are flagged instead of found on paper later.
      • Maintenance, insurance and emergency jobs are tracked to closure in one system.

      cost.md

      Cost benefits

      • 38% lower fuel expenses.
      • Scheduled maintenance replaces breakdowns found late on paper.
