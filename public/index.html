      ~/process · git log --graph

      From idea to production

      The same five stages on every project, each one ending in something you can open and test.

      1. 92f79d7stage 01

        docs(scope): Discover

        We learn the business, the users and the numbers that matter, then agree what version one has to do.

        You get: a written scope and estimate
      2. f3fb837stage 02

        feat(design): Design

        Flows, wireframes and a clickable prototype in your brand, tested before a line of production code.

        You get: a prototype you can click
      3. ecdae0estage 03

        feat(build): Build

        Short sprints with a working build you can open every week, not a big reveal at the end.

        You get: weekly builds and demos
      4. be9a15cstage 04

        release: Ship

        Store submissions, domains, monitoring and a launch checklist, handled with you.

        You get: a live product
      5. 989343fHEAD → mainstage 05

        chore(support): Support

        Fixes, new features and performance work after launch, from the same people who built it.

        You get: a team that knows the code

      ~/faq

      Questions, answered

      What does NXCSOL build?

      SaaS platforms, Three.js and interactive 3D websites, custom business software, iOS and Android apps, Claude Code skills, and 3D rendering in Blender. Most projects combine two, for example a mobile app with the admin dashboard behind it. We also sell our own HRMS.

      How does a project start?

      With a call about what you want to build and why. We follow up with a written scope, a timeline and an estimate. Use the project planner on this page to send us the basics first.

      Can you take over an existing product?

      Yes. We start with a code and infrastructure review, fix what is risky, and then carry on shipping features.

      Where are you based?

      NXCSOL LLC is registered in Kalispell, Montana. Our products are live with teams in North America, Europe, the Middle East, Australia and South Asia, and we work across time zones.

      Do you design as well as develop?

      Yes. We take products from user flows and a clickable prototype through to production, or build from designs you already have.
