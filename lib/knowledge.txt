How a project runs:
- Discover: We learn the business, the users and the numbers that matter, then agree what version one has to do.
- Design: Flows, wireframes and a clickable prototype in your brand, tested before a line of production code.
- Build: Short sprints with a working build you can open every week, not a big reveal at the end.
- Ship: Store submissions, domains, monitoring and a launch checklist, handled with you.
- Support: Fixes, new features and performance work after launch, from the same people who built it.
